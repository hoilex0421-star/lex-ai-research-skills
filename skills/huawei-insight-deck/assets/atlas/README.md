# Atlas source assets

`deck-spec.json` maps H01–H42 to the pinned official cover/navigation/body/ending
layouts. Body content is authored in `../../scripts/build_atlas.mjs`.

The three PNGs were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this illustrative atlas:

- `scenes.png`: 4 × 4 robot-workflow scene contact sheet, 1672 × 941 pixels.
- `people.png`: four fictional project-role portraits, 2048 × 768 pixels.
- `campus.png`: fictional campus overview, 1672 × 941 pixels.

Scene selection is implemented as editable native PowerPoint picture crops.
They are not experimental evidence, real customer sites, or real team portraits.
The wide photo treatments demonstrate placement; replace them with higher-resolution
project-specific imagery for a production report or large-screen presentation.
